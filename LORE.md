## Begining fable
For too long the old monster tribe of self declared Gods reigned supreme. 
Their great cruelty echoed unchallenged through the verses.
They possessed the seeing stone to all worlds buttressed by the pillar of paths.
One world after another fell, ravaged before them. 
Creatures in all places high and low prayed for relief from the monster tribe.
Their pain was met with silence as flesh rotted, cracked, and split - eventually surrendering to the scorn of bones beneath.
Finally one day, the true power above wept as tears rained forming a great flood, washing away the monstrous tribe, sparing only one member. 
This is Bert, freshly unearthed, ready to rid the worlds of hurt.

## Forest Fish 
Through verdant foliage grim with trees whose twisted limbs push out light, we find our hero, Bert, unsure of the world he's provoked and the ills he must face.
He stamps the tender ground with his feet, each pensive step a vote towards danger. 
Every innocent inquiry a beckoning of lethal intent. 
A creature awaits for defiant morsels that challenge its domain. 
Belonging to the river, possessing of a temper, forever in search of a stone.

## Desert Bird
Bert pushes against the gale, determined to mark his path as sanded footprints erase where they're made. 
An angel of death buoyed by winds of malice surveys the desert hellscape. 
A squawking cry echoes from above to specked interlopers below, this shifting swallowing ground can quickly become a grave. 
Guardian of the sky, admonishing dwellers beneath, eyes scope for a particular pebble. 

## Cave Shadow Monster  
Deep within a cave, cold blackened silence is disrupted by the flicker of Bert's torch. 
It's dragon's breath momentarily burns through inky clouds, allowing Bert to see what wishes to hide. 
Lord of darkness, wallows in the belly of eternal night, shadow tendrils claw and weave, fervently inspecting for a rarity, touching each surface of every rock.
